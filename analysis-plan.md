# Preregistered analysis plan

Version 1.0, frozen 2026-07-29 before access to the matched expression matrix.

## Question

In vascular subtypes of aging mouse adipose tissue, does the age-associated
signal attributed to cellular senescence persist after removing genes shared
with IFN-γ response, MHC-II antigen presentation, and TP53-mediated cell-cycle
arrest?

## Eligibility and stop/go logic

Run `audit-data` before reading expression values. Continue only if:

1. the files are from the Xie et al. longitudinal ScaleBio cohort;
2. every cell maps to a non-missing animal ID, age, depot, and vascular
   subtype;
3. age and depot are not confounded with animal;
4. each analyzed age × depot group has at least three animals;
5. raw or count-like expression is available at cell level;
6. vascular subtype labels are available or can be reproduced from supplied
   annotations without inventing labels.

Stop if animal IDs are absent, only pooled group matrices are supplied, or the
files cannot distinguish the four program axes. Do not treat nuclei as
independent replicates.

## Frozen cohorts and endpoints

- Primary tissue strata: SAT and VAT, analyzed separately.
- Primary cells: author-defined endothelial/vascular subtypes represented in at
  least three animals in every included age group.
- Primary exposure: continuous age in months.
- Primary endpoints, computed for each cell then aggregated by
  animal × depot × subtype:
  - full SenCat;
  - SenCat minus IFN-γ;
  - SenCat minus MHC-II;
  - SenCat minus cell-cycle arrest;
  - SenCat residual, minus the union of all three;
  - IFN-γ, MHC-II, and arrest scores.
- Secondary contrast: oldest versus youngest included age group.
- Aggregation statistic: mean cell score; median is a sensitivity analysis.

## Preprocessing and scoring

Use cells that pass the authors' QC and the supplied subtype mapping. Starting
from raw counts, normalize each cell to 10,000, apply `log1p`, then standardize
each gene across eligible vascular cells. Apply the frozen scoring contract in
`signatures/provenance.md`. Report detected-gene coverage for each axis and
stop an axis that fails its coverage floor.

No data-dependent gene selection, manual score-direction flipping, or
post-result redefinition is allowed.

## Animal-level inference

For each depot × subtype × endpoint, fit:

`animal_aggregate_score ~ age_months [+ sex, only if both sexes occur]`

The confirmatory P value is generated by permuting age labels over animal IDs
and preserving each animal's label across all its depots/subtypes. Use every
exact permutation when feasible; otherwise use 9,999 deterministic
permutations with seed 724992. Report the slope, oldest-minus-youngest
difference, 95% bootstrap interval over animals, unadjusted permutation P, and
Benjamini–Hochberg Q within each endpoint family.

Individual animal points are mandatory in figures. Cell-count-weighted
regression is prohibited.

## Predeclared falsification matrix

| Observed pattern | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| full SenCat rises; residual SenCat also rises | broader senescence signal not explained by the three overlapping axes |
| full SenCat rises; residual does not; IFN-γ/MHC-II rise | apparent SenCat association is compatible with inflammatory overlap |
| arrest rises; residual SenCat does not | cell-cycle-arrest component is sufficient; broader senescence not supported |
| IFN-γ/MHC-II rise without full SenCat | vascular inflammatory activation without evidence for broad SenCat activity |
| no endpoint is stable across animals/depots | target hypothesis not supported in this dataset |
| conclusions change under mean/median or one-animal leave-out | fragile/inconclusive |

This matrix prevents a positive story from being chosen after seeing results.

## Sensitivity analyses

- mean versus median animal aggregates;
- mapped/detected coverage thresholds of 20% and 30%;
- leave-one-animal-out slopes;
- oldest-versus-youngest contrast;
- analyses with and without cell-cycle genes in the residual;
- SAT and VAT concordance, without pooling depots.

## Reporting

Report excluded cells/animals and reasons, signature coverage, every tested
subtype, effect sizes with uncertainty, animal points, and all null or
inconclusive results. Separate exploratory results from confirmatory endpoints.
The paper's causal transplantation experiments are context, not proof that a
transcriptomic association is causal.
